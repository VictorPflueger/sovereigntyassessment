# Marktplatz Deutschland Digital (MDD): research notes, no pre-run

Surveyed for version 0.3.0 on 20 November 2026 under decision D3 (TECH_NOTES.md): a pre-run is bundled only where public sources name, for the critical building blocks of most layers, who makes and who runs them.

## What it is

The MDD is the procurement platform that brings together the marketplaces of the Federation, the Länder and the municipalities; it has been in production since March 2026.

## Why there is no pre-run

The public sources found name only its technical basis, the Cloud Service Portal, and not who makes or runs that portal, where it is hosted, or what the marketplace's other building blocks are. A pre-run would record one block with an unknown maker and gaps elsewhere.

Live mode accepts this entry like any other service; its run starts from the sources below.
