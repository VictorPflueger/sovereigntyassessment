# Deutschland-App: research notes

Deutschland-App, researched on 19 November 2026: the Bundestag's report on the Federal Government's answer (hib, October 2026), the city of Wiesbaden's announcement as a pilot municipality, a heise report and a netzpolitik.org article on the companies involved, SAP's corporate governance statement, the AusweisApp's page, Wire's company page and the BMDS slides from the IT-Planungsrat's congress.
