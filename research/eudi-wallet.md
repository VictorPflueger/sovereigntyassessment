# EUDI Wallet “d-you”: research notes

EUDI Wallet “d-you”, researched on 20 November 2026: a Born City report on the start of d-you, the project's pages on the project and on the digital ID, two heise reports on the prototype and on the design, netzpolitik.org's questions and answers, the bundled reference facts on app distribution (verified at build time), Apple's page on its affiliated companies and Google's terms of service. The wallet is not in operation yet, so the assessment describes the plans the sources state.

Every quote below was read on its page with the web fetch tool on the date given, and the pre-run data/runs/eudi-wallet.js cites it. tools/check-runs.mjs sets each quote's verified flag from this file: a quote counts only when it is listed here, verbatim, under its URL.
