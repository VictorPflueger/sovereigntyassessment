# Serviceportal.NRW: research notes

Serviceportal.NRW, researched on 2026-11-27 for version 2.1.0: the state's ordinance on the portal and its amendment of December 2025, two announcements of the state government on the new Nordrhein-Westfalen-Portal, the portal's imprint, a press release of the state government of 2023 on signing in with the BundID, a case study and an organisation page on drupal.org, IT.NRW's statute, the Drupal Association's FAQ, the BundID's imprint, a contractor's post on the portal's first phase and the imprint of the forms platform's maker; for the starting points, the product sheet of the federal content management system and a press release of ekom21. The case shows that the method applies to a state's platform as it does to a federal one.

The name: the ordinance writes "Serviceportal.NRW"; the site meineverwaltung.nrw calls itself "NRW Serviceportal". The state announced on 6 March 2026 that a landing page of the new Nordrhein-Westfalen-Portal is to replace it, and presented the new portal at a press briefing on 19 November 2026, shortly before its start; no page read gives the start date or the new portal's address. A company and the people who presented the portal at that briefing are not recorded: the page does not say what they build or run.

Every quote below was read on its page with the web fetch tool on the date given, and the pre-run data/runs/serviceportal-nrw.js cites it. tools/check-runs.mjs sets each quote's verified flag from this file: a quote counts only when it is listed here, verbatim, under its URL.

### [GV. NRW. 2020 S. 1212](https://recht.nrw.de/gvnrw/2020-s1212)
Read on 2026-11-27 (German). The ordinance of 10 December 2020 that introduced the Serviceportal.NRW-Verordnung (Artikel 1). The page writes the name without a space after the dot throughout.

> „Serviceportal.NRW“ ist ein Verwaltungsportal im Sinne von § 2 Absatz 2 des Onlinezugangsgesetzes.
> Verordnung über den Betrieb und Ausgestaltung des Serviceportals.NRW gemäß § 5 a des E-Government-Gesetzes
> Die für die Bereitstellung und den Betrieb des Serviceportal.NRW zuständige Stelle ist das für Digitalisierung
> ermöglicht Serviceportal.NRW dem Nutzer die Einzahlung dieser Gebühr über ein elektronisches Zahlungsverfahren

### [GV. NRW. 2025 S. 1138](https://recht.nrw.de/gvnrw/2025-s1138)
Read on 2026-11-27 (German). The amending ordinance of 8 December 2025; it rewrites § 1 (1) and extends a deadline in § 12 from 2025 to 2030.

> Das Serviceportal.NRW dient dabei als Dienstleistungsportal zur Bereitstellung von elektronischen Verwaltungsleistungen
> Verordnung zur Änderung der Serviceportal.NRW-Verordnung
