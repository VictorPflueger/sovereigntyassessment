# Mein Unternehmenskonto (MUK): research notes

Mein Unternehmenskonto, researched on 20 November 2026: its information page, the Bavarian administrative regulation on the operating conditions of the organisation account (in the Bavarian ministerial gazette and on gesetze-bayern.de), the BayernPortal's page on ELSTER, the data protection notice for Mein Unternehmenskonto, ITV.SH's guide to registering ELSTER organisation certificates, the AusweisApp's page about Governikus, a CIO DE report on Dataport and the BMDS paper on the Deutschland-Stack.

Every quote below was read on its page with the web fetch tool on the date given, and the pre-run data/runs/muk.js cites it. tools/check-runs.mjs sets each quote's verified flag from this file: a quote counts only when it is listed here, verbatim, under its URL.

Read on 2026-11-20 (German).
