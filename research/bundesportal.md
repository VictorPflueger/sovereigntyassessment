# Federal Portal (Bundesportal): research notes

Federal Portal (Bundesportal), researched on 19 November 2026: § 9a of the E-Government Act, the BMDS paper on the Deutschland-Stack, a news item of the German Bundestag on a parliamentary answer (December 2020), the BundID's imprint, slides of the BMDS from the IT-Planungsrat's congress, and the bundled reference facts on the Federation's IT (verified at build time). The portal's own pages need JavaScript and could not be read with the fetch tool.

Every quote below was read on its page with the web fetch tool on the date given, and the pre-run data/runs/bundesportal.js cites it. tools/check-runs.mjs sets each quote's verified flag from this file: a quote counts only when it is listed here, verbatim, under its URL.
