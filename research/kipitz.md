# KIPITZ (federal AI platform): research notes

KIPITZ, researched on 20 November 2026: the Bundestag's news item on the federal administration's use of AI, the bundled reference facts on the Federation's IT (verified at build time), a CIO DE profile of the ITZBund's cloud and AI programme, a survey by Öffentlicher Dienst News, a heise report on AI in the federal administration and a ChannelPartner report on the Federation's AI project.

Every quote below was read on its page with the web fetch tool on the date given, and the pre-run data/runs/kipitz.js cites it. tools/check-runs.mjs sets each quote's verified flag from this file: a quote counts only when it is listed here, verbatim, under its URL.
